Weight-loss FAQ

Is the medication included?

Yes. Your monthly price includes medication if your provider prescribes it, plus check-ins and labs.

Do I need insurance?

No. Our pricing is self-pay and the same every month.

How much can I expect to lose?

Clients on GLP-1 programs often lose 10–15% of body weight over 6–12 months with lifestyle changes. Results vary.

Are there side effects?

Some people feel nausea at first. We start low and adjust slowly.

Can I combine it with aesthetics?

Yes. Complete members save 10% on Botox, filler and IV therapy.
